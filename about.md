## Our websites

**wopta.org is owned and operated by {{ site.org.name }}.** The name stands for *West Orange PTAs*: this domain is Mount Pleasant PTA's own site and also hosts a [shared calendar](/calendar/) for PTAs across the West Orange Public Schools district. ## Get involved

Join the PTA at your child's school. Membership letters go home in the first weeks of September; if you did not get one, ask the school office or find your school's PTA on Facebook. Any contribution of time that is comfortable for you makes a difference.

- Mount Pleasant families: [join or renew]({{ site.org.official_site }}) and [say hello on Facebook]({{ site.org.facebook }}).
- Questions for the PTA: [{{ site.org.email }}](mailto:{{ site.org.email }}).
- Other West Orange schools: the [{{ site.org.council_name }}](mailto:{{ site.org.council_email }}) can connect you with your school's PTA leaders.
