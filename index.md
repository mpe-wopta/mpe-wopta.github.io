# Mount Pleasant PTA

Welcome! We are the volunteer parent-teacher association for {{ site.org.school }} in West Orange, New Jersey. This site is our simple home on the web; membership, the store, and the current officer list live on [our original site]({{ site.org.official_site }}), and day-to-day conversation happens in [our Facebook group]({{ site.org.facebook }}).

**wopta.org** stands for *West Orange PTAs*. Beyond Mount Pleasant, this domain hosts a [shared calendar](/calendar/) for PTAs across the district, and we intend to offer each school's PTA a home under it (for example `mpe.wopta.org` for Mount Pleasant Elementary).
